With the Pro package ($250), you send us pre-cut clips or an existing video and we edit it into a professional highlight video. With the Elite package ($550), you send us raw game links and we do everything: watching the footage, finding the best moments, and creating the complete highlight video from scratch. The Elite package is fully done for you.

We work with all the main game footage platforms — Veo, Hudl, Pixellot, and any other match recording software your club uses. Just send us the links and we'll handle the rest. If you're unsure whether your footage will work, send us a sample and we'll let you know.

The Pro package has a 2 to 5 business day turnaround. The Elite package takes 7 to 10 business days because we're reviewing full games. If you have a deadline (upcoming trial, transfer window), let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Yes, both packages include unlimited revisions. We'll keep refining until you're completely happy with the result. There's no cap on changes and no extra charge.

You'll receive a YouTube link (public or unlisted, your choice) plus a downloadable HD file sent via email. This means you can share it with coaches directly, embed it in player profiles, or send it to clubs anywhere in the world.

Football is our specialty and where we do our best work. We understand the game, the positions, and what coaches are looking for. We occasionally work with other sports, but football is our focus.

Most of our clients are players aged 14 to 21. The window where new opportunities open up. Whether they're trialling for higher-tier clubs, aiming for state selection, or exploring overseas pathways, we can help.
